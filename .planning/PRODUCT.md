# PRODUCT — Lex

> The "who and why" every Qualia agent reads before designing or building. Generated by `/qualia-new` from discovery answers; update as the product clarifies.

## Register

```
register: product
```

Lex is a tool. Design serves the product (earned familiarity is the bar). Lawyers will live in this 30–90 minutes per day — density and speed beat flourish. The marketing/landing dimension does not exist for the demo (we hand Fotini a logged-in workspace, not a homepage).

## Users (specific, not personas)

1. **Fotini Kandri** — Cyprus lawyer, runs solo practice in Nicosia handling divorce + immigration cases. Files docs to iJustice. Reaches for Lex to generate invoices and receipts for clients, track billable hours per case, and ask the AI assistant which clients are overdue — without leaving her workspace.

2. **Marios Christodoulou** — solo practitioner in Limassol, corporate + property law, mid-50s. Reaches for Lex on Friday afternoons to generate the week's invoices, review the monthly financial summary, and queue payment reminders for late-paying clients on Monday.

3. **Elena Papadopoulou** — paralegal at a 4-lawyer firm in Nicosia, mid-30s. Reaches for Lex every morning to log billable hours for the partners, draft invoices from those hours, and check which retainers need topping up.

## Brand voice

```
Voice: Precise. Quiet. Respectful of expertise.

Voice in motion:

  Empty state (Invoices):
    "No invoices yet. Ask Lex to draft one —
    'Invoice Andreou for the divorce filing, €450, due in 14 days' —
    or open a blank template."

  Confirmation:
    "Invoice #2026/0014 sent to maria.andreou@gmail.com.
    Receipt will post on payment."

  Error:
    "Couldn't read that PDF. The file may be password-protected —
    try unlocking it, or paste the text directly."
```

No exclamation marks. No "Awesome!" No "Oops!" No emoji. Reads like a competent paralegal speaks to the lawyer they work for: short, factual, professionally respectful.

## Anti-references (3)

- **Clio / MyCase / generic US legal SaaS** — gradient-heavy, cluttered nav, "trusted by 150,000 lawyers" social-proof shouting. Lex must feel quiet and confident, not salesy. The product earns the lawyer's trust through restraint.
- **iJustice / typical Cyprus government portal** — gray tables, 1998 form fields, no hierarchy. Lex is the antidote to this. We will be judged against this baseline; we must look from 2026.
- **ChatGPT-style "single chat box in the middle of the page"** — Lex is a workspace, not a chat toy. The AI lives **inside** the work (sidebar command bar; per-record actions), not **as** the work.

## Positive references (≤3)

- **Linear** — keyboard fluency, tabular density, restraint with color. The way the app stays out of the way.
- **Stripe Dashboard (financial sections)** — how numbers render: tabular, right-aligned, hierarchical without shouting. Aspirational standard for the invoicing tables.
- **Things 3** — calm typography, generous whitespace at the margins, dense where it matters. The empty states.

## Strategic principles

- **The numbers are the product.** Tabular numerals, right-aligned, sortable. Anything that distracts from reading a column of money fast is wrong.
- **Bilingual is first-class, not localized.** Greek and English have equal status in the UI from line one. Greek strings are not afterthought translations of English copy — they are written native, and many invoice templates render both side-by-side.
- **The AI lives inside the work.** Command-bar (⌘K) on every screen, per-record actions, never a separate "chat tab." Asking is faster than clicking, but clicking still works.
- **Trust ledger is sacred.** Client funds NEVER appear in revenue views. The two are visually distinct (different surface tints, different iconography, different table chrome) so a lawyer can never confuse them at a glance.
- **Cyprus VAT is non-optional.** Invoice numbering, tax IDs, VAT lines are not configurable — they're required and they're correct. The product enforces compliance.

## Constraints

```
Framework:         Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19
Component library: custom (DESIGN.md tokens) + Radix primitives where useful
Performance:       LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1
Accessibility:     WCAG 2.2 AA
Browsers:          evergreen (Chromium, Safari, Firefox), mobile-first
i18n:              Greek (el) + English (en), GR default
PDF render:        server-side (no client-side WASM)
Data residency:    EU (Supabase Frankfurt or Ireland)
```
