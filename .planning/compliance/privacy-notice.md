## (a) Who we are

This privacy notice describes how `[FIRM NAME]` ("we", "the firm",
or "the data controller"), a law firm registered in the Republic of
Cyprus with registered office at `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`, processes
your personal data when you engage us for legal services or interact
with our billing systems.

For questions about this notice or your personal data, contact us at
`[FIRM CONTACT EMAIL]` or by post at the address above. Our designated
contact for data-protection matters is `[DPO OR EQUIVALENT CONTACT]`.

## (b) What data we collect and why

We collect the personal data necessary to identify you, deliver legal
services, raise invoices, accept payments, and comply with the tax and
professional-conduct obligations imposed on Cyprus law firms. The
categories are:

- **Identification:** your legal name (and the name of your authorised
  representative where you act through one), postal address, email
  address, and telephone number.
- **Tax identification:** your VAT identification number and, where
  relevant, your Cyprus tax identification number.
- **Billing data:** invoice line items describing the legal work
  performed for you, the amounts charged, dates, and payment references.
- **Payment data:** the bank-transfer reference or other payment
  identifier used to settle invoices. We do NOT store full card
  numbers; card payments (where offered) are processed by our
  payment partners.

Collecting this data is necessary for the performance of our engagement
contract with you and to comply with our statutory obligations as a
Cyprus-licensed law firm.

## (c) Legal basis

We process your personal data under the following GDPR legal bases:

- **Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)** — processing is necessary to perform
  the engagement contract between you and the firm, including raising
  invoices and accepting payment.
- **Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)** — processing is necessary to
  comply with Cyprus tax law, including the Assessment and Collection
  of Taxes Law (N.4/1978, as amended) and Cyprus VAT Law L.95(I)/2000,
  and with the professional-conduct rules applicable to Cyprus advocates.
  `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: confirm statute reference with tax counsel.`
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)** — limited to the
  administration of our practice (e.g. internal accounting, security
  monitoring) where this does not override your fundamental rights.

We do NOT use your personal data for marketing without your prior
explicit consent.

## (d) Recipients

We share your personal data only with the following categories of
recipient:

- **Our service provider (Qualia Solutions Ltd.)**, which operates the
  Lex billing and trust-accounting application on our behalf as a
  data processor under a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
- **Sub-processors engaged by Qualia Solutions Ltd.**, listed in the
  sub-processor register maintained by the processor. Categories
  include hosting, database, AI-assisted text processing, transactional
  email, and uptime monitoring providers.
- **The Cyprus Tax Department** and other competent Cyprus authorities,
  where disclosure is required by law or by lawful request (including
  audit, VAT inspection, and judicial process).
- **Our banking partners**, when you make payment, for the purpose of
  reconciling the payment with the corresponding invoice.
- **Our professional advisors** (accountants, auditors) under
  confidentiality.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to any third party.

## (e) Retention

We retain records subject to Cyprus tax retention obligations as
follows:

> **Records subject to Cyprus tax retention obligations (invoices, receipts, accounting records) are retained for ten (10) years from the end of the relevant tax year, in accordance with Article 8 of the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law (N.4/1978, as amended) and Cyprus VAT Law L.95(I)/2000.**
>
> `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: confirm statute citation with tax counsel before publication.`

Personal data that is NOT covered by a statutory retention obligation
is retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it
was collected, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

When you ask us to delete data that is subject to the ten-year retention
period above, we will restrict its processing (Article 18 GDPR) instead
of deleting it, and we will inform you of the legal basis for the
restriction.

## (f) Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights in respect of your
personal data:

- **Access (Art. 15)** — you can request a copy of the personal data
  we hold about you.
- **Rectification (Art. 16)** — you can ask us to correct inaccurate
  data.
- **Erasure (Art. 17)** — you can ask us to delete your data, subject
  to the retention obligation above.
- **Restriction (Art. 18)** — you can ask us to restrict processing
  in defined circumstances.
- **Portability (Art. 20)** — you can ask us to provide your data in
  a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
- **Objection (Art. 21)** — you can object to processing based on
  legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at `[FIRM CONTACT EMAIL]`.
We will respond within one month, extendable by two further months for
complex requests (with notice to you).

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint
with the **Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of
the Republic of Cyprus** at `[CYPRUS DPC CONTACT EMAIL]` or via the
DPC's official website.

## (g) Cookies and tracking

The firm's billing portal (the Lex application) uses one strictly
necessary cookie: the **authentication session cookie**, which keeps
you signed in for the duration of your session and is deleted when you
sign out or when the session expires.

We do NOT use marketing cookies, third-party advertising trackers,
analytics cookies, or any non-essential cookies in the billing portal.

If our public-facing website (separate from the billing portal) uses
analytics or other cookies, those are described in a separate cookie
banner shown on first visit.
